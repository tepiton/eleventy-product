## Why we exist

In 2021 the four of us worked on a product team that made decisions by
whomever argued loudest in a meeting. The data existed - it always
exists - but getting an answer took a data request, a two-day wait, and
a chart nobody trusted by the time it arrived.

{{ metadata.title }} is the tool we wanted then: questions answered in seconds,
numbers everyone can check, and no waiting on anyone.

## How we work

- **Warehouse-first.** Your data stays where it lives. We read it, we
  do not copy it, and we never hold it hostage.
- **Opinionated on purpose.** Fewer charts, better defaults. If a
  feature needs a manual, we usually just remove the feature.
- **Small surface, fast product.** We ship weekly and tell you exactly
  what changed, in plain language.

## What we will not do

We will not sell your data, wrap core metrics in a higher tier, or
publish a pricing page that requires a sales call to decode. The free
plan is genuinely usable, because we remember being on the other side
of that.
