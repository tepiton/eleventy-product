items:
  - q: "How long does setup take?"
    a: "Most teams see their first events within ten minutes. Warehouse connection is a credentials form; the snippet is one copy-paste."
  - q: "Do I need to know SQL?"
    a: "No. Northlight's query builder covers the questions teams ask daily. If you do know SQL, you can drop into it and back out without losing your place."
  - q: "Where does my data live?"
    a: "In your warehouse, always. Northlight reads it there and caches aggregates only. We never take a full copy."
  - q: "What counts as an event?"
    a: "Any tracked action - a pageview, a click, a server-side call. The Monday digest and your invoice both use the same count, so there are no surprises."
  - q: "Can I export everything?"
    a: "Yes. Every chart exports to CSV, and your event schemas are available via the API at any time. Your data is not a hostage."
